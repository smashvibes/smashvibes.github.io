===============================================================================
THREE THINGS WE CHANGED FROM THE ORIGINAL FIELD LIST
===============================================================================

1. WE ARE NOT COLLECTING IC / NRIC NUMBERS.
   Date of birth already proves age eligibility, and we can check photo ID at
   the desk on the day without writing the number down. Singapore's PDPA rules
   on collecting NRIC numbers are strict, and a shared Google Sheet is the wrong
   place to keep them. Same checks, far less risk.

2. WE ADDED PARTNER DETAILS.
   Three of our four categories are doubles. The original list covered one
   player only, so for a pair we would have had no partner name, age or contact
   - we could not have verified eligibility or built the draw.

3. PHOTO / VIDEO CONSENT IS OPTIONAL, NOT A REQUIRED TICK.
   We record matches and share clips. Consent that cannot be refused is not
   consent, so it is a clear yes/no and does not block entry. Anyone who says no
   stays out of published footage - someone needs to own that on the day.

FIVE OPEN QUESTIONS - WE NEED DECISIONS
===============================================================================

1. AGE CUT-OFF. We have set "under 17 on the event date" = born on or after
   15 November 2009. The other common rule is "under 17 as at 1 January 2026".
   These give different eligibility lists. Which one?

2. ENTRY FEE. The tournament page does not mention one. If there is a fee, the
   form needs a payment step and a "payment made" question, and the flow
   changes. Is entry free?

3. MEN'S vs MIXED DOUBLES. The page says "Men's Doubles & Mixed Doubles
   combined", "2 groups of 5 pairs". We have made them two separate choices of
   5 pairs each. Is that right, or is it 10 pairs across one combined draw?

4. U17 SINGLES - ONE DRAW OR TWO? The prize list shows a single "Singles
   Under 17" prize, but we collect gender. Is it one open draw of 16, or
   separate boys' and girls' draws?

5. WHO OWNS THE RESPONSE SHEET? It will hold minors' names, dates of birth,
   contact numbers and parents' numbers. It should be shared with the smallest
   possible group, and deleted after the event. Who is responsible?
