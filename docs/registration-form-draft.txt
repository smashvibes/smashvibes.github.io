===============================================================================
THREE THINGS WE CHANGED FROM THE ORIGINAL FIELD LIST
===============================================================================

1. WE ARE NOT COLLECTING IC / NRIC NUMBERS.
   Date of birth already proves age eligibility, and we can check photo ID at
   the desk on the day without writing the number down. Singapore's PDPA rules
   on collecting NRIC numbers are strict, and a shared Google Sheet is the wrong
   place to keep them. Same checks, far less risk.

2. WE ADDED PARTNER DETAILS.
   Three of our four categories are doubles. The original list covered one
   player only, so for a pair we would have had no partner name, age or contact
   - we could not have verified eligibility or built the draw.

3. EVERY DECLARATION BOX MUST NOW BE TICKED.
   A "required" checkbox question in Google Forms only demands ONE tick, not
   all of them - so our declaration lists could have been submitted with a
   single box checked. They now carry validation that requires every box.

4. PHOTO / VIDEO CONSENT IS OPTIONAL, NOT A REQUIRED TICK.
   We record matches and share clips. Consent that cannot be refused is not
   consent, so it is a clear yes/no and does not block entry. Anyone who says no
   stays out of published footage - someone needs to own that on the day.
